Selling starts before learning

Trial pressure and enrollment prompts surface within minutes, before meaningful learning happens. The strongest part of the product appears after onboarding, so commitment feels premature.

Language creates friction

Chapters are English first. Students who learn best in another language hit a comprehension wall before they reach the value. Confusion replaces curiosity, and they lose confidence early.

The opportunity · enter the product manager

Before confusion begins, speak their language.

1

Language picker at onboarding

Screen 1 of the first session asks for a preferred language for explanations, then stores it on the profile (today it shows class and grade, but no language). One question, one screen, zero friction added.

Metric% of users choosing a language other than English: a proxy for latent demand
2

Bilingual notes first, dubbed audio second

The Learn tab already has Notes. Translating concept notes is low cost (text, not a new recording) and ships faster. Watch tab videos can add a language toggle in a later sprint.

MetricNotes open rate by language segment, against the English only baseline
3

Doubts answered in their language

A language aware doubt chat with LLM powered responses: ask in Hindi, get the answer in Hindi. Achievable with LLM tooling today, and ALLEN's brand trust does the rest.

MetricSupport ticket volume drop, and session length increase after launch
