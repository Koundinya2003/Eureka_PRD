# Eureka by ALLEN — Product Teardown

A first-hand product teardown of Eureka, ALLEN's Class 10 board-prep app. 
Looks at the gap between the product's strong pedagogy and its weaker 
onboarding/monetization design, and proposes a localization fix.

## Contents
- `Eureka_Unlocking_Magic_Presentation.pdf` — 7-slide visual teardown covering 
  onboarding, pedagogical strengths, the friction points (premature upsell, 
  English-only content), and the recommended opportunity.
- `1_Page_report.pdf` — 1-page written summary: product understanding, 
  biggest strength, biggest weakness, and the one recommendation.

## TL;DR
Eureka's story-led, narrative-driven lessons are its strongest differentiator 
in Indian EdTech. But the funnel asks for commitment (trial/enrollment) before 
students experience that value, and English-first content creates a 
comprehension wall for non-English-first learners. The recommendation: 
let students experience the product in their language before asking them to commit.
